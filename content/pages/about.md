I'm Varun. I make videos about hardware and games at [@therevprogamers](https://www.youtube.com/@therevprogamers), write code that occasionally turns out to be useful, and put the long-form version of both here.

This site exists because video is a bad archive. A number mentioned in minute fourteen of a build video is effectively lost — you can't search it, skim it, copy it, or correct it later. Text fixes all four.

## What I publish

**Reviews** of hardware I've used long enough to have a real opinion about. Every one carries a score out of ten, an explicit what-works/what-doesn't split, and a spec table. Long-term use, not unboxing impressions.

**Build guides** for things I've actually done and would do again — with the reasoning attached, not just the steps.

**Project write-ups** for the code I publish. The repos are public; the posts cover the decisions and trade-offs rather than just the finished result.

**A gear list** that gets updated when my setup changes, rather than rewritten quarterly for engagement.

## The rules

- **No sponsored verdicts.** Sponsorships pay for videos, never for opinions. No brand sees a review before publication and no brand changes a score.
- **Affiliate links are labelled.** They're marked inline and disclosed at the top of any page carrying them. They cost you nothing and don't influence what makes a list — the two items I criticise hardest in [my gear post](/blog/gear-i-actually-use/) have affiliate links too.
- **Free hardware is declared** in the post, at the top, not buried in a description.
- **Corrections stay visible.** When I'm wrong, the correction is dated and stays in the post rather than being quietly edited away.
- **Numbers come with methodology.** A benchmark without its conditions is decoration.

## How the site works

Everything you're reading is a Markdown file in a [public Git repository](https://github.com/Varun-SV/TheRevProGamers). No CMS, no database. A build script turns the files into static HTML, and the video grid, project list and supporter walls pull themselves from the YouTube, GitHub, Patreon and Buy Me a Coffee APIs on a schedule — so nothing here is a number I typed once and forgot to update.

If you want the full architecture, including the two things that didn't work, [I wrote it up](/blog/building-this-blog/).

## Getting in touch

Corrections and "you're wrong about X" are genuinely welcome — the repository takes issues and pull requests, and a typo fix is a perfectly good first contribution.

If any of this has been useful, [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/VarunSV) and [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/varunsv) are how the hardware gets paid for.
