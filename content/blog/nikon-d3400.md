Every year someone tells me this camera is obsolete. Every year it keeps producing photographs I'm happy with.

The D3400 is an entry-level DSLR from a lineup that no longer exists, with a mount the manufacturer has moved on from, no in-body stabilisation, no articulating screen and video specs that were unambitious when it launched. On paper, a phone from three years ago beats it in most measurable ways.

It also still takes photographs that my phone cannot.
