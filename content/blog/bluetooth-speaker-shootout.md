Nobody sets out to own eleven Bluetooth speakers. They accumulate — a sale here, a review unit there, a "this one's small enough to just keep in the bag" that turned out to be the fourth one small enough to just keep in the bag.

The upside of an accidental collection is that I can do something most reviews can't: put all of them on one desk, play the same tracks through each, and rank them against each other rather than against a memory of something I returned eight months ago.
